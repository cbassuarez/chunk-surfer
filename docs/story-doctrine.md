# Story and HUSH doctrine

## Ground truth and readings

Ruled with Seb, 2026-11-04. This section outranks everything below it. The plan is `docs/plans/last-minutes.md`.

**Ground truth is a likelihood.** He most likely dies in the van. A clean run, where he exits Ellery and drives off, stays possible. All endings are superimposed under that as interpretations of it, and they do not generally sublate the clean run.

**Every component is overdetermined on purpose.** One thing can be explained by several mechanisms at once, classically surreal and dreamlike. The Surfer is or maybe isn't HUSH, pressure, music itself, source code, the previous recordist, Sarah/Lyle and the protagonist, all at the same time.

**So:**

- No presented line or image may kill a reading. Test a new line by what it rules out, not by what it fits.
- The game never asserts the ground truth. It shows physical evidence, and that evidence should support more than one reading.
- A voice may doubt the clean run. Nothing may disprove it.
- The rest of this document is one reading, plus naming rules for code. `chunkSurfer`, `borrowedRecordist` and `playerShadow` are handles, not verdicts on what those things are. Their separateness in code does not settle their separateness in the story.

**The meaning-making cascade.** As his faculties fall, meaning rises. Ordinary things become signs: a plastic bag a spirit, the sun a light at the end of a tunnel, the moon a blood moon. Not spiritualism: neurons failing in front of him, de-inhibition, gamma surges, the themes of end-of-life dreams and visions. This is essence, not accident. It is also one mechanism among several, so the game never says "it was only a bag" and never says it wasn't.

**Known about him:** the blue-ink notes in the cab are his own. "There was someone I meant to play it for" and "For the part that wouldn't play" are Lyle/Sarah, and the text does not choose between them. Why he took the job is deliberately open.

## Stable rule

HUSH is older than music. Ordinary music is harmless. Repeated exact attention can make diffuse acoustic density cohere into temporary, nonverbal agency; recordings, compulsory reference listening, performance pressure, and charged institutions make that coherence easier to sustain.

The student identified by the transfer label `CHUNK SURFER` is the `chunkSurfer`: a listener worn into a vessel by compulsory correction and reference work. The prior contractor is the `borrowedRecordist`, a separate body later used as a reference. A recorded prior player is always the `playerShadow`; code and documentation use `playerShadow`, `shadowFrames`, `shadowPlayback`, `causalTape`, and `causalAnchors` for that concept.

HUSH has no body, face, dialogue, narrator, or stable internal voice. It is never a `who` value. The building and institution can act independently. Chunk Surfer traces may repeat recorded speech, take calls, corrections, quotas, and institutional language, but may not supply a new explanatory monologue.

## Three passes

1. A first story run presents trustworthy recordings and impossible effects without confirming a cause.
2. THE HUSH re-enters that exact filed night and enacts its required causes. Missed causes are corrected at their canonical time without introducing another visible entity.
3. Later story returns mark prior knowledge and make physical evidence easier to recognize, but the evidence must be reacquired in the current run before it grants leverage.

Optional records can explain the mechanism and improve combat, Source guidance, or the borrowed-recordist route. They never gate ordinary completion. Source Space expresses relationships spatially; it does not resolve the ontology in an explanatory paragraph.

## Signal ownership

Every presented line has a semantic `signalRole`: `human`, `playerShadowReturn`, `chunkSurferTrace`, `building`, `institution`, or `unattributed`. `who` is only a display label.

Player-noise perception accepts player-generated diegetic sound and allowed microphone activity. The 168 BPM battle performance, architectural resonators, program playback, score, and room leakage are non-player audio. They cannot reveal the player, spoil a take, or feed the player-noise meter.

One exception, and it is physical: a source standing in the room being recorded. The practice wing's metronomes are non-player audio, and a room tone with a clock ticking in it is not a room tone, so their ticks reach the take by level and can spoil it. They still cannot reveal the player, never fetch anything after him, and are quieted by hand or by shutting their rooms in. Nothing says who keeps them running.

## The unresolved name

The gate guard asks for the operator's name and visibly accepts two answers. Rain masks both written presentations and accessibility captions print `[NAME OBSCURED]`. When a Steam display name or OS username has been separately enabled, the sanitized value may be synthesized locally beneath those two booth lines; it remains runtime-only and the repeat is clearer. Without a permitted value, the voice uses the stable obscured-shape syllables. Persistence records only `operator.name.received = true`. No literal operator name is permitted in saves, tapes, captions, transcripts, replay data, diagnostics, telemetry, or exports, and the B3 pre-roll echo never repeats it.

## Endings

The contract organizes repeated attention; it did not create HUSH. Demolition can cover a downbeat without ending the inherited process. The guard's intervention remains human. Reversed pre-roll can contain exits before either door sounds. The coffee explanation is a filed interpretation, not omniscient truth. Releasing the borrowed contractor does not destroy or reveal HUSH and never adds a sixth story ending. Driving off is not an ending either. It is what closes them: first person, then third, then black, then credits, with nothing on screen saying what it means. The Other Recordist closes the same way on foot: you and Alan walk to the van as the game fades, and you fade before him.
