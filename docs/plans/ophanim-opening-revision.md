# Ophanim opening: staging and verification

Scope: the existing encounter, from its motorway introduction through the
return to driving. Preserve the authored words, optional inquiries, ambiguous
causes, existing assets, and renderer.

## Reproduced baseline

The live production opening shows the school before its invitation to look,
then moving motorway paving and boundaries cross its forecourt/frontage. The
school is appended in a cab-normalized frame on every frame. Its facade spans
the road corridor, and the older scenic elevation is much deeper than the
facade alone. This is spatial intersection, not evidence of a near-plane bug.
The windshield view is clear to distant scenery between wipes. The prologue
uses modest rain, low extinction, and a slow shared wiper period.

All opening player lines currently bypass speech permission, and the host
automatically advances every `me` line. Some are echoes of selected answers;
others are new replies within a continuing exchange. Those meanings need to
be explicit, independently of the number of available options.

## Acceptance evidence

Measure transformed bounds from the actual GLB accessors against the road's
swept bounds, including long waits and bends. Record production gameplay with
mixed audio before and after, disclose accelerated versus natural passages,
and inspect full wiper cycles. Compare a few speech-rate candidates using
short and long original lines; keep the shared voice identity unchanged.

Test fresh single replies, selected-answer echoes, empty/multiple banks,
repeat input, pause/review/skip, enlarged captions, reduced motion and optional
returns. Technical tests and recording completion do not establish independent
player comprehension, listening quality or comfort.
