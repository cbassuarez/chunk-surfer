# Earthfall: the quake, the fall, the waking, the landing

Earthfall was a trapdoor: a step forward, three seconds of hairline cracks on a
still camera, a quiet fall through an empty shaft, a twenty-two second room
that slid into a point, and a landing that held nothing but the exit. This is
the beat board for making it an earthquake that takes the town down with you,
and a landing that shows something.

## Approved corruption direction: torn address space

The walk and the full depth of the fall contain fixed tears in the world:
flat, irregular sheets, long stretched triangles, black absences and silver
printed remnants with hyperchromatic cut planes. They originate along the
plaza's actual failure branches and crowd the spaces between the broken
coffer, soundboard and binding levels. Corruption should read as the
desecration of the construction, with the surviving ground and falling
building breaking around it.

The user selected concept A and specified that the faults are **2D and fixed
relative to the world**. They have zero thickness, both visible faces, and
immutable world vertices. They never billboard, drift, rotate, follow the
camera, receive the earthquake transform, or move with released terrain.
Their silhouettes remain behind as the construction fails. The existing
terrain, support and landing simulation continues to own physical footing;
the fault sheets do not introduce hidden collision walls.

The layout contains 72 forecourt tears, 120 fixed cut faces over the upper
ground, 480 faults around the whole town, 640 above it and 640 through the
100-metre depth, including larger remnants at the three existing strata.
The surrounding volume extends beyond the town in every direction and up to
230m in world height. Corruption fills the ground, horizon and overhead view
from the beginning of the walk. It keeps a clear central fall lane.
Twelve material/stage batches carry the 1,952 sheets
through the ordinary opaque depth/acquisition renderer. Silver stock retains
physical engraved texture; unstable cut matter uses magenta, cyan, violet
and lime. Black sheets retain absence without emission. The selected concept
and the alternative studies are saved under
`artifacts/earthfall-corruption-options/`.

## Raw material

- **Ground motion.** 2019 Ridgecrest Mw 7.1 (2019-07-06 03:19:53 UTC), station
  CI.CLC (China Lake, ~11 km hypocentral; S–P 1.40 s), channels HN[ENZ] at
  100 Hz from the SCEDC FDSN dataselect service. Counts divided by each
  channel's published sensitivity, demeaned, detrended, 3% taper, 4-pole
  zero-phase Butterworth 0.3–5 Hz; displacement by double integration,
  re-filtered. Band-limited PGA 0.28 g N–S, 0.16 g E–W, 0.10 g vertical;
  peak displacement ~4 cm. Energy share in 0.5–3 Hz: 48–61% by axis.
  Envelope: quiet → P at 1.5 s → S at 2.9 s → strong motion peaking 0.29 g at
  9 s → decay through 20 s → tail to 31 s.
- **Implosions, stepped frame by frame.** Radio Network House, Christchurch
  (Commons, 25 fps) and the AfE-Tower, Frankfurt (Commons, 240 fps). Both show
  the same order: charges first, visible only as short horizontal dust jets at
  the lowest floors (AfE also at a mid-height ring); then ~0.5 s in which
  nothing moves; then the core goes and the upper block drops almost rigidly,
  walls drawn after it (RNH tilts toward the side cut first; AfE telescopes
  straight down); the block stays whole until it reaches the dust; dust boils
  out at the base and rolls outward for 10+ s.
- **Story.** `docs/story-doctrine.md`: Source expresses relationships
  spatially and never explains the ontology. The landing reveals two atlas
  sectors together (Seb, 2026-10-30): PREVIOUS CONTRACTOR (`borrowedRecordist`)
  and BODY RETURN (`borrowed-body-return`).

## Motion rules

- Ground motion is the record, scaled for the screen (horizontal peak ~12 cm,
  rocking up to ~1.5°), applied to the world around the eye. The camera's
  input path is untouched: no lag, no delay, no smoothing on the player's look.
- Buildings respond as damped single-degree-of-freedom oscillators driven by
  the record's acceleration (5% damping; low buildings ~2 Hz, the false Ellery
  ~0.9 Hz), so they lag and overshoot the ground the way real buildings do.
- **Reduced motion:** no world rocking or translation and no building sway;
  the P jolt and S waves are carried by sound, dust and the tear alone. The
  buildings still fall and break.
- **Flashes:** none. The charges are dust and sound; nothing exceeds three
  luminance flashes per second.

## Sound rule

The rumble is derived from the record: low-passed noise whose level follows
the record's envelope and whose surges follow its waveform, so what is heard
and what moves are one signal. Material clips (restraint, failure body, stone
spall) are triggered by the record's jerk peaks and by each break-up stage.
No score.
