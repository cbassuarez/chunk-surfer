source material. Unequal compressed courses, chipped ends, recessed upper
fragments and an asymmetric crest replace the enclosing service building.
The existing lift, receiving ledge and their mechanical geometry stay intact.

The bank retains its 84 by 16 metre support and 3.55 metre underside. The
highest fragment reaches 18.35 metres. All above-ground courses finish before
the first free forecourt; the lower support meets Braided Divide at 4.2 metres.
Its 27 closed prisms share their exact polygons with collision and rendering.

An eight metre mouth opens into a widening, open-sky cleft. One oblique tongue
creates a shallow leftward turn. The field releases as the body clears its real
eye-height sightline, rather than after a distance quota. The same geometry
restores the shore when returning. No camera turn, relocation or movement lock
is applied. The route remains reversible and allows free lateral movement.

Actual source-atlas lines form low-contrast material grain at a fixed world
scale. The cut silhouette carries the distant view; fine print is available
under close light. The finite carried flashlight affects surfaces and leaves
the surrounding nothingness empty. Both sides use the same geological mesh,
including the visible cut ends and closed underbelly.

Inside Source, the cut material accounts for the recording's higher exposure
with a received-light shoulder. Close torch light retains the layers and source
grain. The exterior shore presentation retains its original exposure.

The Braided Divide field, its nineteen original models, its two crossings,
the listening court, voluntary warned battle and grey-door exit retain their
existing ownership. Routes beyond the grey doors are outside this change.

Validation covers closed outward hulls, supported upper fragments, finite
