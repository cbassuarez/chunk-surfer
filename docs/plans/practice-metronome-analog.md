## Three experience claims that can fail

1. **An instrument before an answer.** Unfamiliar players discover tempo changes and different ways to touch the mechanism before reading it only as a stop switch. Action availability is verified; human inference remains pending.
2. **One physical cause.** Weight position, projected contact, pendulum travel, click spacing, recorder events and saved state agree during editing, drift, catch/release, pause and reload. The room's response follows those same heard events. Tests and native captures cover specific transitions; a cross-system check with the actual recorder verifies that equal-rate clocks still interrupt the take and that explicit physical stops permit a clean full-duration take. Actual perceived clarity still needs unfamiliar-player review.
3. **Honest failed theories.** Six running clocks at equal BPM remain audible and do not award quiet. Stopping clocks or shutting their actual rooms still permits the existing student beat. Experimentation does not erase earlier accomplishments.
