# Apparition family — grounded shadows and residual charge

Design decision study, 13 October 2026. No game asset or runtime is changed by this study. These are three versions of the user's governing idea: **shadow/energy people; the shadow is zapped into the ground; energy is left behind.** They are alternatives for existing appearances, not proposals for new encounters.

## What exists and what is failing

The seven `apparition_pose_*` meshes in `tools/chunk_surfer/build-props.mjs` are cylinders, boxes and straight beams. Their 1.7 m adult envelope and common foot pivot are useful contracts, but the trunk ends horizontally, arms terminate as rods, the head often has no neck, knees are straight segments, and almost no pose shows a planted support leg. The existing August review captures in `artifacts/apparitions-review/space-hall.png`, `space-natatorium.png`, `space-tower.png`, and `continuity-substitution.png` show this clearly. Those captures are historical reference, not verification of the current executable.

The current renderer also gives most visible apparitions a strong white emissive surface and a small **white practical light** (`APPARITION_PRESENTATION` and `apparitionLights` in `src/game/emergency-light-runtime.js`). That makes the body the bright answer to its own shadow. The selected realignment should reverse the hierarchy: the held figure is a dark human-shaped interruption of red light, the floor takes that shadow on a legal beat, and a restrained residue of charge shows where the person-shaped absence stood. This is a material and event change, not merely a new mesh.

The good part is the event system. `src/game/apparition-director.js` assigns three distinct initial postures, can hold one body still, reorient it, remove it without closing the formation, substitute a pose in one place on a new light beat, show only its cast shadow, delay the body behind its shadow, and occasionally hard-reveal it. `src/game/emergency-light-runtime.js` places them around a *room practical*, within a 2.2 m no-loom distance, rather than spawning them on the camera. Five authored formations cover the foyer threshold, Hall, natatorium, academic corridor, and tower landing in `src/data/apparition-staging.js`. `src/render/props3d.js` suppresses a `shadowOnly` body from colour while keeping its geometry in the isolated practical-light shadow pass. The main scene then takes the shared world/VFD dither path. This is the expressive structure the redesign should preserve.

The same seven poses are borrowed by dark, quiet recording hallucinations (`src/game/recording-hallucinations.js` and `src/main.js`), the basement watcher (`head_turn`), and the Tower Lost synchronized player/Surfer bodies. The Hall Three combat view uses *separate 2D sprites* that echo head-turn, stoop, and arm-out. `stair_shadow_figure` is a separate cast-only occluder; `player_shadow_figure` is a separate HUSH/prior-operator silhouette and the invalid-pose fallback. The ordinary ending bodies are not apparitions. A production pass must inspect all these dependents, but it must not make them interchangeable characters or add appearances.

## Research to design inference

Atkinson et al. tested facially obscured whole-body expressions in full-light and point-light moving and still displays. Body posture alone conveyed information, and movement improved recognition over still form. This supports modelling shoulder slope, pelvis, knee bend and hands inside the shadow contour, then letting the *timing of held and changed poses* do the uncanny work. It does not prove any particular horror treatment. [Primary study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1068/p5096?download=true).

Johansson's biological-motion experiments showed how sparse, coordinated joint motion can read as a person. Here the design inference is that even energy residue can remain human if its marks preserve joint relationships and one credible support foot. The current director's abrupt beat changes should not be smoothed into a walk cycle. Sparse **static** residue is a harder recognition problem than moving point lights, so it must be tested at actual gameplay distance rather than assumed to work. [Original study](https://doi.org/10.3758/BF03212378).

An occlusion experiment found that cast-shadow relationships affect how human viewers complete partially hidden shapes and judge depth. This supports making the held figure and cast shadow derive from the *same closed 3D geometry*, especially for the shadow-only beat, rather than using an independent wall decal. It is a depth-perception result, not evidence that any specific shadow is frightening. [Primary experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2911394/).

## Shared production contract

- One anatomically continuous human-shaped **occluder** template with distinct neck, sloped shoulders, upper and lower arms, palms, thighs, knees, calves and feet. It reads from front, three-quarter and side as a plausible person without skin, face, clothes, hair, gender cues, insignia or identity. This is neither an actor in a black suit nor a skeletal creature.
- Seven frozen, hand-authored poses retain the semantic IDs and bounds. Quiet poses stay genuinely quiet; `head_turn`, `arm_out`, and `symmetric` remain rare accents. Each pose has one load-bearing leg and a believable pelvis/ankle response. Hands have mitten-scale silhouettes with a thumb, not individually articulated fingers that vanish at in-game resolution.
- Keep a foot-level pivot, 1.55–1.82 m height, and enough limb thickness to survive the lower-resolution practical shadow map and shared VFD dither. A held figure and its cast shadow use the same pose and footprint. `shadowOnly` continues to suppress the visible occluder while keeping the actual geometry in the practical-light shadow pass. No independent wall sprite or albedo cutout is used to fake the cast.
- Reserve the downward zap for a selected **absence or substitution** event on a legal red beat, not every ordinary pulse. It must resolve through the load-bearing foot and a floor contact, not lift or launch the figure. The dark mass goes into the floor; a separate, short-lived, shadowless residue retains the old pose at the old station, then dies away. It is a render consequence, not a fourth apparition, light source, collision, minimap contact or interactable.
- Substitution remains a *cut* to one complete new posture, not a limb morph or walk. The old-pose residue may be perceptible for a fraction of a beat, but cannot obscure which of the three stations changed. Stillness must truly freeze. Absence leaves the station empty once the residue clears. Pure shadow-only cards have no visible energy body; delayed reveal can bring one in later. The rare hard reveal should intensify charge structure, not restore a blinding white person and white companion light.
- Review the same pose in Hall red, natatorium darkness, tower near field, torch on/off, visible/shadow-only/hard reveal, and actual recording footage. Keep current reduced-dread suppression and reduced-effects continuity. Recheck capture output, not just live preview; an older report documents that saved apparitions were once absent but did not diagnose the cause.
- Put the charge residue into the same world/VFD/dither path as the figures and footage; avoid a clean, full-screen neon overlay that would break continuity or disappear from recordings. Material parameters may differ by direction, but the current `.82` white emissive and per-body `apparitionLights` should be explicitly reevaluated. Keep the red practical as the primary light and the shadow map as the spatial proof.
- Update the Hall Three 2D sprites to echo the chosen *pose contours*, while keeping their separate combat roles and fused-chair origin. Give the stair occluder comparable human shadow anatomy without making its body visible. Give the HUSH fallback a parallel anatomical quality pass only if that separate identity's current rendering calls for it; do not replace the prior operator with one of the seven crowd bodies. Ordinary ending bodies remain their own cast. Add no new apparition locations.

The seven existing pose IDs should become an authored silhouette score, not seven procedural offsets of one rigid mannequin:

| Pose ID | Anatomical action that must read at room distance |
| --- | --- |
| `neutral` | One foot fully accepts weight; the other knee loosens, one shoulder drops, hands rest at different heights. |
| `side` | A real quarter-turn through ribs and pelvis, with one shoulder occluding the other and a clear side-profile calf. |
| `stoop` | Bend at hip and ankle, not by translating the head forward; the supporting leg and reaching hand explain the lean. |
| `head_turn` | Clavicles stay still while neck and blank head turn together; it reads as attention, not a face reveal. |
| `arm_out` | Scapula, elbow, wrist and open palm form a human gesture; its exact target stays uncertain. |
| `weight_shift` | Pelvis and ribcage counter-rotate over a new support foot; bent free knee gives a readable before/after cut. |
| `symmetric` | The only unnaturally even stance: both feet planted, shoulders level, hands held alike. Its rarity makes it an accent. |

At the intended gameplay distance, spend geometry on shoulder and elbow breaks, the gap between thighs, foot contact and the thumb-side of each hand. Spend none on facial topography, fingers or cloth microdetail. The held occluder must survive front and side light; energy alone cannot carry the human reading because `shadowOnly` removes the visible surface entirely.

## Direction: Grounded Negative

**First reading: a human-shaped loss of light.** The held figure is nearly black, with one hairline ivory corona only where the red practical catches its contour. It is plainly a person-shaped occlusion rather than a person in black clothing. On an absence or substitution beat the shadow mass pulls straight down through the weighted foot. Branching dark-red current briefly spreads into the floor. What remains upright is a *nearly complete* thin charged contour in the exact old posture, while the cast can linger for a beat and then clear. This is the most legible and literal version of “shadow into ground, energy left behind.” The fully readable residue gives the player time to recognize the old pose before the new one appears. **Strength:** clear, memorable transformation and excellent pose readability. **Risk:** a complete bright outline may read as a generic glowing ghost or a fourth figure; it needs a very short lifetime and no independent lamp.

## Direction: Vacancy Circuit — recommended

**First reading: someone occupies a gap in the room.** The figure is a dense, opaque negative with only a few dull charged marks *inside* the human silhouette at shoulder, wrist, hip, knee and support foot. The cast shadow remains ordinary and continuous. On the selected beat, narrow current routes from those marks down one load-bearing leg and drives the black mass into the floor. The upright remnant is deliberately incomplete: a small constellation at the old joints and hand positions, no full halo or body fill. The viewer supplies the absent person. **Strength:** strongest uncertainty, compatible with the existing dither language, and least likely to become a bright ghost. **Risk:** sparse energy could become invisible at gameplay distance or in saved recordings. The contact sheet should be judged at native resolution and the charged marks enlarged only enough to survive that test.

## Direction: Borrowed Cast

**First reading: the wall knows a person the room does not contain.** A human-shaped cast is the initial apparition, with no visible source in the aisle. On the selected red beat its dark shape appears to peel down the wall and run along the wall-floor junction into a single grounding point. The wall is left bare; a delicate upright energy figure now occupies the absent person's station in the room. It carries the same posture and support foot that the wall shadow had. This uses the current `shadowOnly` capability as the opening of the phenomenon rather than an occasional variant. **Strength:** most surreal spatial rule, and the cleanest use of shadow-only appearances. **Risk:** it depends on readable receiving surfaces; natatorium, foyer and tower compositions must prove a useful wall, chair bank or floor cast without a fake projection. The physical occluder and energy successor must still share pose and coordinates.

## Selection and acceptance

Choose by the part of the event that should dominate: **the full downward zap and human outline** (Grounded Negative), **the uncompleted person in scattered charge** (Vacancy Circuit), or **the cast becoming a person-shaped field** (Borrowed Cast). I recommend Vacancy Circuit: it makes the shadow a convincing human presence, gives the ground a clear role, and leaves enough absent information for the player's imagination. Borrowed Cast is the bolder architectural option if its cast surfaces prove reliable in all five staged spaces.

The three image-generated concept plates in `artifacts/apparition-family-design/concepts/` use the existing Hall screenshot as a setting reference. Each is a **three-beat illustration**—held, grounding, remnant—not an in-engine capture or proof that current lighting supports the effect. The shared built-in image-generation prompt requested a consistent red-black concert hall triptych with no HUD or text and a plausible faceless human shadow; the direction prompts specified respectively a complete charged contour, sparse internal circuit and joint constellation, or a shadow-first wall-to-floor transfer. Their source prompts deliberately exclude costume, exposed anatomy, monsters, smoke and spectacular lightning.

After selection, build the production asset and prove it in actual game captures. A successful handoff has: all seven pose names mapped; no block limbs at front/profile; distinct bent knees, shoulders, hands and support legs; the same closed occluder in the colour and practical-shadow passes; a single station grounding on a legal event beat; its energy residue in the shared dithered world and recorded footage; pure shadow-only without visible residue when the director requests it; a hard reveal that does not become a white lamp; no new locations or contacts; and matched coverage in recording hallucinations, the basement watcher, Tower Lost and Hall battle views. The historical screenshots, generated plates and source review above are design evidence, not acceptance proof.
