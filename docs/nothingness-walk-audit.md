## Earlier polish directions

These directions preceded the walkable-realm pass. Several are now implemented above; retain the others as candidates for playtest-led refinement.

7. **Make chapel arrival an audible return.** Match the leaked building bed to the actual corridor heard after crossing. Let rain and snow fall away at the threshold, then allow one ordinary building sound to settle. Test the complete walk without jumps before changing the final pause or narration.
8. **Carry one consequence forward.** A restrained later inspection or room-specific memory of the warm jumper could give the optional home lasting weight. This is new narrative scope; choose its meaning before adding an ending bonus or mechanical advantage.

## Defects to repair first

### The home can be lost by exploring the doors in order

**High priority. Reproduced in the runtime.** The generator guarantees an initially openable door for every realm, but it does not preserve a whiteout door until the player earns the home visit. An ordinary whiteout crossing consumes its door immediately, just like any other crossing.

For the review seed `4417`, the open doors in walking order are glass storm, burning, inverted, inverted, whiteout, flood, glass storm and two moons. The sole whiteout door is number 7, before the required flood and sand relics. Crossing the open doors as encountered leaves five relics and no door home. Seeds 5 and 40 also reproduce the problem. The loss survives runtime reconstruction from the saved state; unlocking home afterward leaves zero available whiteout doors.

Keep the home reachable regardless of discovery order. Preserve or restore an eligible whiteout threshold, let its scar become the home entrance, or otherwise make this access durable. The solution should preserve the pleasure of doors being taken back. [Initial realm coverage](/Users/seb/chunk-surfer/src/game/source-space-runtime.js:5466), [door consumption and home eligibility](/Users/seb/chunk-surfer/src/game/source-space-runtime.js:6279), [five-relic gate](/Users/seb/chunk-surfer/src/main.js:7734).

### Reload can consume a doorway without granting its reward

**High priority. Reproduced state mismatch; reward timing confirmed in code.** Crossing saves `ROUTE_DOOR_ERASED` immediately. Main grants the relic later, when it observes the transient crossing become null. The crossing, its pending reward and `stationWay.walk` are not serialized. Reload in between preserves the consumed door and loses the pending visit. A realm with only one opener can then never provide its relic. Reload during the home walk similarly risks consuming its whiteout doorway before the jumper is awarded.

Make consumption, pending reward and recovery agree. A saved pending crossing should either resume, settle its earned reward, or return an available doorway. This should cover reload during entry, hold, fold and the home window, without duplicate rewards. [Immediate erasure](/Users/seb/chunk-surfer/src/game/source-space-runtime.js:6285), [transient crossing](/Users/seb/chunk-surfer/src/game/source-space-runtime.js:6264), [reward on crossing completion](/Users/seb/chunk-surfer/src/main.js:7749).

### Source teardown leaves station audio running

**High priority for presentation. Confirmed call-path omission; audibility needs a live listen.** `clearSourceRuntime()` stops the street, shore and bell audio, but does not stop the side-edge bed or the realm beds. The normal chapel handoff calls this function and switches to building rendering. Cleanup for those beds exists in `syncSourceRender()`'s no-runtime branch, while the ordinary Source tick returns early once the runtime has gone. The steady rain/breathing and last nearby realm gains can therefore survive into the chapel. The run-audio reset also omits these graphs.

Stop and reset every station sound and transient presentation at the runtime's actual exit, including `stationWay` and any home tableau. Verify both the chapel crossing and a debug/new-run exit. [Runtime teardown](/Users/seb/chunk-surfer/src/main.js:7155), [otherwise unreachable cleanup](/Users/seb/chunk-surfer/src/main.js:8110), [chapel handoff](/Users/seb/chunk-surfer/src/main.js:8485), [Source tick guard](/Users/seb/chunk-surfer/src/main.js:17292).

### The saved chapel checkpoint has no position

**Medium priority. Reproduced fallback defect.** The reducer creates `chapel-outskirts`, but `checkpointPosition()` has no corresponding case. It falls back to hall `(0,0)`, where chapel-phase geometry supplies no floor. Normal full saves retain their coordinates, so this is a fallback or positionless-save recovery defect rather than a claim that every reload fails.

Give the checkpoint a valid post-tape position, and exercise recovery with coordinates absent. [Checkpoint assignment](/Users/seb/chunk-surfer/src/game/chunk-surf-state.js:842), [position resolver](/Users/seb/chunk-surfer/src/game/source-space-runtime.js:1690).
