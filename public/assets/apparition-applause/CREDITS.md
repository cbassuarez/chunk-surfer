# Applause source credits and changes

Hand samples: FreePats World Percussion, hand clap recorded by Roberto in June 2019 with an AKG condenser microphone. CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Source: https://github.com/freepats/world-percussion and https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Percussion/world-and-rare-percussion.html . The source README states raw recordings were cropped, edited and processed. The four released samples retain their complete 0.316–0.403 s duration; mono 48 kHz, common gain, conditional final 4 ms cosine taper only. No attack filter/gate, pitch shift or time stretch. One documented recordist; distinct performers are not documented.

Ensemble: Dorian CLAIR, Applause 1 (2363), https://bigsoundbank.com/applause-1-s2363.html . CC0 1.0. Actual indoor applause, ensemble size unknown. Original published 7.2 s mono 48 kHz 24-bit WAV retained in source materials; processing history unknown. Runtime uses exact 0.825–2.375 s excerpt, common gain and 6/14 ms cosine edge fades.

Meeting response: Thomas McKenzie, Sebastian J. Schlecht, Ville Pulkki, A dataset of measured spatial room impulse responses for the transition between coupled rooms, v1.2 (2021), https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4636068 . CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Selected meeting-room trajectory measurement 30 cm, W/channel 0, original file timing retained, 0.52 s window, 40 ms end taper, unit discrete energy gain.

Kiln response: Jack Smith, Juan García, Hashim Riaz, Pau Rosell; Audiolab, University of York / OpenAIR, Hoffmann Lime Kiln (2017), position 2, https://www.openair.hosted.york.ac.uk/?page_id=518 . CC BY 4.0. W/channel 0, resampled 96 to 48 kHz, original file timing retained, 0.88 s window, 40 ms end taper, unit discrete energy gain.

Ordinary uses meeting W. Treatment uses 0.85 meeting + 0.14 kiln W. Light sends preserve the recorded hand body and existing indoor ensemble decay. No invented hall tail, synthetic crowd, dynamic normalization or limiter. Treatment additionally mixes an antialiased 18 kHz / 11-bit parallel branch at 12%, leaving 88% original clean waveform. Ordinary stays clean. The exact baked PCM derivatives are shared by runtime, reference and native Live playback. One constant gain matches reference integrated levels. Timing, reflection send and treatment-only processing all differ: this is a full-package comparison, not an isolated DSP experiment. No creator endorses this game. Original files, source pages, SHA-256 records and license evidence accompany the work under artifacts/apparition-applause/materials-v2 and materials/v2. Source recognition was confirmed by the user; final cue listening and fresh-player validation remain pending.
